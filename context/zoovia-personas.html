Kennel Operators — the supply side
👩‍🌾

Pat Webb

Meadow View Kennels, Shropshire · 14 runs · 22 years
The Veteran
58 years old Sole trader 4-star AAL No staff
How she operates today
Leather-bound diary she calls "the Bible." Excel for income tracking. WhatsApp for updates to worried owners. Takes deposits by bank transfer — checks her banking app twice a day to match incoming transfers against diary entries. Vaccination books in a lever-arch folder on the reception desk. Been doing it this way for two decades and it works — mostly.
Pain points
  • Enquiry chaos at Christmas booking season — phone, Facebook Messenger, website form, and walk-ins all arriving at once, no way to keep track
  • Chasing deposits manually — sending reminder texts, checking the banking app, updating the diary, one by one
  • Finding a vaccination record at 7am drop-off when the folder is at the back of the office
  • Council inspection anxiety — knows she should have better records but the current system is all in her head
In her own words
"I've been doing this for 20 years. I don't need a computer telling me which dog goes in which run."
"I heard of a kennel that lost all their Christmas bookings when their software crashed. No thank you."
"My busy season is so hectic I can't stop to learn something new. It has to just work."
Adoption trigger A missed enquiry that cost her a booking, a compliance flag at inspection, or — most likely — a trusted peer kennel owner telling her they switched and it was fine. Trust travels through the local kennel network.
Zoovia pitch to Pat "Works exactly like your diary. Just doesn't sit on the counter, and the deposits chase themselves."

Gary Patel

Paws & Stay, Hertfordshire · 22 runs + daycare · 8 years
The Grower
41 years old Ltd company 2 full-time staff Ex-Revelation Pets
How he operates today
Expanded from 10 to 22 runs in 2022. Tried Revelation Pets for three years, abandoned it after two data loss incidents and a 60% price hike. Now running a bodged system: Calendly for booking requests, a Google Sheet for occupancy, WhatsApp group with staff, and his own head for everything else. Paying £60/mo for software he no longer fully trusts.
Pain points
  • Staff see different information to him — double-booked twice last year when a staff member confirmed a run he'd already allocated
  • Deposit tracking is a mess — each staff member manages it differently, he has no single view
  • Revenue leakage — extras like medication admin, extended stays, and additional walks are rarely billed consistently
  • Deep scepticism born from being burned before — won't adopt something new without proof it's reliable
In his own words
"I don't mind paying if it actually works. I'm already paying for something that doesn't."
"My team need to be able to check things without ringing me at 7am."
"I've been burnt before. I need to see it working before I move everything across."
Adoption trigger The next double-booking — or hitting the point where managing three systems takes more time than a proper tool would cost. Needs a free trial or a clear migration path from his current setup.
Zoovia pitch to Gary "One system your whole team works from. Every booking, deposit, and dog record in one place — and it doesn't go down."
Dog Owners — the demand side
👩‍💻

Anna Clarke

Marketing manager, Surrey · Cockapoo called Milo · First-time boarder
The Anxious First-Timer
34 years old Pandemic dog owner Boards 1–2x/year
Her current experience
Googled "dog kennels near Guildford." Found four websites — two with no prices listed, one phone number that rang out, one online form she filled in and didn't hear back from for three days. Eventually booked by phone after calling four places. Doesn't know if the kennel she chose is actually licensed. Deeply anxious about leaving Milo for the first time.
Pain points
  • Can't verify which kennels are legitimate — no easy way to check licence status or star rating
  • No prices visible upfront — has to enquire just to find out if she can afford it
  • Paper-based onboarding — asked to bring vaccination card to drop-off (forgot it, had to go back)
  • Zero contact during the stay — no photo, no update, no way to know Milo is okay without ringing and feeling like a nuisance
In her own words
"I just want to know he's okay. One photo a day, that's all I need."
"I couldn't find any reviews I actually trusted. Google reviews could be anyone."
"How am I supposed to know if it's a proper licensed kennel? There's no way to check."
Adoption trigger A kennel that shows its AAL licence and star rating on its listing, lets her complete Milo's profile digitally, and sends her a photo on day one. She will recommend Zoovia to every dog owner she knows — this audience is evangelical when trust is earned.
Zoovia pitch to Anna "See which kennels are licensed and rated. Book in minutes. Get a photo update every day."

Frank O'Brien

Sales director, Manchester · Two Labradors · 8–10 stays/year
The Pragmatic Regular
52 years old Travels for work Same kennel 6 years
His current experience
Has a trusted kennel he's used for six years. Books by WhatsApp message, pays by bank transfer. The kennel knows his dogs well — dietary requirements, that Barley is scared of other dogs at feeding time. The relationship works. But he has to book far in advance with no way to check availability without ringing, and he's occasionally caught short when his usual dates are already gone.
Pain points
  • No online availability — has to message or ring to check if dates are free, which he can't always do during the workday
  • Repeats the same dog info every visit — no stored profile, fills out the same form each time
  • Confirmations are WhatsApp threads — can't easily forward a booking reference to his PA or add to his calendar
  • Occasionally caught out — peak dates gone before he's got round to booking
In his own words
"I just need to get it done quickly. I'm not fussed about the details."
"Why can't I just check availability online at 11 o'clock at night when I'm planning my trips?"
"I've been going there for years. They know the dogs. I'd just like the admin to be easier."
Adoption trigger His kennel joins Zoovia and suddenly the availability is live, the booking takes two taps, and the confirmation lands in his inbox with a reference he can forward. He adopts immediately and without fanfare — and probably doesn't even think of it as a new product, just "the kennel got better."
Zoovia pitch to Frank "Check availability. Book in two taps. Your dogs' details are already there."
